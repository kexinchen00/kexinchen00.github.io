Research

My research uses game theory and information economics to study strategic information acquisition, revelation, and collective decision-making. Building on this work, I aim to understand how AI reshapes information incentives and decision outcomes in human-AI systems, with a particular interest in mechanism and information design.

I also study machine learning theory, particularly generalization and knowledge acquisition in neural networks.
