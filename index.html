Kexin Chen

Ph.D. Candidate, CUHK-Shenzhen

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer and Information Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. My research uses game theory and information economics to study strategic information acquisition, information revelation, and collective decision-making, with a particular interest in how AI reshapes information incentives and decision outcomes. I also study machine learning theory, especially generalization and knowledge acquisition in neural networks.
