Kexin Chen

I am a Ph.D. candidate at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. My research spans game theory, information economics, and decision making, with an interest in how AI affects information incentives and decisions in human-AI systems. I also work on machine learning theory, particularly feature learning dynamics and generalization in neural networks.

I am currently seeking research positions and welcome conversations about shared research interests and potential collaborations.
